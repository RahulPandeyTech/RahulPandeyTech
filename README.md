<h3>5. Enterprise Governance, Identity & Management Hierarchy</h3>

<h4>Cloud Adoption Framework (CAF) Methodology</h4>
<p>Microsoft CAF guides end-to-end cloud adoption across six iterative phases:</p>
<ol>
  <li><b>Strategy:</b> Define business justification, motivations, and expected ROI.</li>
  <li><b>Plan:</b> Inventory workloads, assess organizational readiness, and plan landing zones.</li>
  <li><b>Ready:</b> Prepare the cloud environment, management groups, and core identity foundations.</li>
  <li><b>Adopt:</b> Migrate and modernize workloads in agile sprint cycles.</li>
  <li><b>Govern:</b> Enforce Azure Policies, cost guardrails, and compliance benchmarks.</li>
  <li><b>Secure & Manage:</b> Continuous monitoring, threat detection, and automated patching.</li>
</ol>

<h4>Tenant & Management Group Hierarchy</h4>

<div align="center">
  <img src="./assets/animated-tenant-hierarchy.svg" width="100%" alt="Tenant & Management Group Hierarchy with Animated Electric Arrows" />
</div>

<h4>Best Practices:</h4>
<ul>
  <li><b>Subscription Boundaries:</b> Segregate subscriptions strictly by environment (<code>Dev</code>, <code>UAT</code>, <code>Prod</code>) and function (<code>Platform</code> vs <code>Application</code>) to prevent noisy neighbor problems and isolate the blast radius.</li>
  <li><b>Azure Policy & Initiatives:</b> Assign built-in initiatives (e.g., CIS Azure Benchmark, MCSB) at the Management Group level; enforce audit/deny effects with time-bound, documented policy exemptions.</li>
  <li><b>RBAC & PIM:</b> Group-based RBAC with Microsoft Entra ID; mandate Privileged Identity Management (PIM) with just-in-time access, MFA, and multi-stage managerial approvals.</li>
</ul>

<h3>6. Enterprise Hub-and-Spoke Networking Architecture</h3>

<div align="center">
  <img src="./assets/animated-hub-spoke-network.svg" width="100%" alt="Enterprise Hub-and-Spoke Networking Architecture with Animated Laser Arrows" />
</div>

<h4>Key Networking Elements:</h4>
<ul>
  <li><b>IP Address Management (IPAM) & CIDR:</b> Comprehensive discovery and auditing of on-prem CIDRs to prevent overlapping IP address spaces with Azure VNets.</li>
  <li><b>Centralized Traffic Inspection:</b> User Defined Routes (UDRs) forcing <code>0.0.0.0/0</code> and inter-spoke traffic through the Azure Firewall / Network Virtual Appliance (NVA).</li>
  <li><b>Controlled Egress & Ingress:</b> Centralized Internet egress through firewall with TLS inspection; inbound traffic filtered via Azure Application Gateway / Front Door with Web Application Firewall (WAF).</li>
  <li><b>Private Connectivity:</b> Private Endpoints for all PaaS services (Key Vault, Storage, SQL) with central Private DNS Zone resolution linked back to the Hub VNet.</li>
</ul>
